==================== 5. closing paragraph (POST-HOC, descriptive; no verdict changes) ====================
Only early vs polished states were available; no intermediate iterates or per-iteration loss components were saved.
In the synthetic runaway, the loss decrease is shared between the two terms, with the smoothness penalty supplying
somewhat more of it.
- Across the 20 runaway models (correlated P0/P1 plus independent P0/P1 seeds 0 and 4):
  - the total loss falls by a median 13%;
  - the D2 penalty falls x0.25 (4x; correlated P0 x0.20, i.e. 5x), while the training MSE falls only 5%;
  - the D2 term supplies a median 0.61 of each model's decrease (pooled 0.49), the data term 0.37 (pooled 0.48).
- In the certified P0/P1 models (independent seeds 1-3, 5-7), the D2 term falls less (x0.62) and the data MSE by 2%.
- The growth goes into what the cell does not penalise:
  - Q (the RBF null space) grows x34-x40 in the runaway cells, against x1.0-x1.1 in P2;
  - in correlated P0, where SiLU is unpenalised, the raw SiLU size grows x85;
  - in P1, where SiLU is penalised, SiLU shrinks and Q still grows x34.
- Overfitting in the runaway models: 12 of 20 lower the training MSE while the in-distribution test MSE (against the
  noiseless teacher) rises. That is all 8 correlated-P0 models (test +5% to +88%) and 2 of 4 independent ones, but
  only 2 of 8 correlated-P1 models; P1 more often improves both.
Real-data comparison:
- The brief's "penalty fell 8-88x, data term changed 1-5%" pairs numbers from two different real-data segments.
  - From 107 epochs to capped, the penalty fell x8-x88, but the data term also fell 43-53%. The data term then
    carried 0.64-0.83 of the total-loss decrease.
  - From capped to A final, the data term fell 1-5%, but the penalty changed only x0.84-x1.41 (before/after). The data
    term then carried 0.80-1.05 of the decrease.
- What matches: in both settings the penalty falls by a large factor (synthetic 4-5x, real 8-88x) while the data fit
  improves by a few percent along the late, unregularised path (synthetic 2-8%, real capped -> A 1-5%).
- What differs: in the synthetic runaway the penalty's fall is a larger share of the loss decrease (about half to
  two-thirds) than on real data (0.17-0.36 from 107 epochs to capped; about 0 after capping). On real data the penalty
  was a smaller part of the loss to begin with, and the data term still had far to fall.
- So the synthetic runaway is penalty-driven to a larger degree than the real one. In both, the growth sits in
  directions the penalty does not see.
