==================== closing paragraph (POST-HOC, descriptive; no verdict changes) ====================
Where the runaway lives. In the synthetic 4-3-1 model the runaway is mainly a layer-2 phenomenon. In the correlated
cells, layer 1 takes only 11.0% (P0) and 2.4% (P1) of ||delta theta||^2 from early to polished (medians, 8 seeds each).
The rest goes into the three output edges psi_j(tanh s_j): their affine RBF part (P0 0.338, P1 0.888), their SiLU
weight (P0 0.550, P1 0.000) and the output bias (0.013 / 0.083). The curved RBF part of every edge takes <= 0.002.
This makes the runaway a growth of near-linear output edges that cancel each other: layer-2 cancellation
sum_j RMS(psi_j)/RMS(sum_j psi_j) rises from 1.90 to 3.22 (P0) and from 1.96 to 3.09 (P1). Layer-1 cancellation also
rises, but in P0 only (all four edges 1.72 -> 3.29; within a correlated pair 1.24 -> 1.84, between the two pairs
1.29 -> 1.41); in P1 it rises less (1.83 -> 2.32).
Concentration. Growth is not confined to one hidden unit: the largest unit takes 0.58 (P0) and 0.66 (P1) of the
change, an effective 2.1 and 1.9 of the 3 units. The x1/x2 pair takes 0.75 / 0.63 of the (small) layer-1 change;
because every input in this design belongs to a correlated pair, no "rest" exists to compare against.
Saturation. Saturation is essentially absent: |tanh s| > 0.95 on <= 0.3% of training rows (cell medians; the maximum
for any model is 3.0%), and |s| > 3 on 0%.
What separates unbounded from finite growth (independent P0). The two never-certified seeds (s0 x703, s4 x170) put
99.4% of their change into layer 2 (layer-1 share 0.006, against 0.241 for the six certified seeds, which grew x9.4).
They are also the only independent-P0 seeds whose cancellation grows: between-pair 1.31 -> 2.12 against 1.47 -> 1.22
for the certified seeds. The certified seeds' layer-2 cancellation falls from 1.75 to 1.18. The two uncertified seeds
differ from each other. s4 runs away through layer-2 cancellation (3.61; L2 affine 0.52 + L2 SiLU 0.44). s0 has
little cancellation (layer 2 1.24) but puts 0.37 of its change into the output bias next to 0.59 in the affine RBF
part of the output edges. This is consistent with offset trading between the output bias and the near-constant part of
the psi_j, since tanh s lies in (-1, 1). That reading is not tested here.
Difference from the hackathon model. In the hackathon KAN-delta, the runaway was cross-edge cancellation in the
FIRST layer: the cross-edge ratio rose from 1.7-2.9 (AdamW) to 4-10 (capped / A final), alongside substantial
hidden-unit saturation (|s_j| > 3 on 12-39% of rows for the seeds) and ||theta||^2 growth of 150-1550x.
The two models share the affine (null-space) character: the hackathon RBF null-space share was >= 0.994, and the
synthetic layer-1 RBF energy is 0.999 (P0) and 0.982 (P1) affine at polish. Both also show little within-edge
SiLU/RBF cancellation (hackathon 1.13-1.33).
The synthetic runaway differs in three ways. (i) It is driven from layer 2, not layer 1. (ii) Its layer-1 cancellation
stays at the bottom of the hackathon range (median 3.29 in correlated P0, 2.32 in P1). (iii) It needs no saturation.
Collinear inputs are enough to open the flat direction, but in this small, unsaturated network that direction is
mostly taken up by the output edges cancelling each other, not by first-layer edges cancelling across inputs.
