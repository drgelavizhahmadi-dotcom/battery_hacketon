## Purpose and legend

Every claim the paper makes, with its evidence and status. Provenance rule: every number in this table has a committed source; illustrative derivations are committed as dated notes before they are cited; derived numbers carry the raw values they come from. Process-record rows carry no numbers or empirical assertions; one that acquires either moves to an evidential label. A claim that is not in this table does not go into the paper. Numbers and commit hashes are as reported in the study logs; the repository files win over this table where they differ.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| PR-held | A pre-registered prediction that held |
| PR-failed | A pre-registered prediction that failed; the claim is what the failure shows |
| PR-two-sided | A pre-registered prediction with no direction; the verdict is the pre-fixed branch that applies |
| PR-descriptive | Measured in a pre-registered study but not itself a prediction |
| Exploratory | A pre-registered arm with no predictions attached |
| Post-hoc | Defined after seeing results; exploratory |
| Post-outcome diagnostic | A descriptive diagnostic run after the relevant outcomes were known |
| Descriptive (plan committed first) | From a descriptive study whose analysis plan was committed before computing; no predictions |
| Descriptive (no plan) | Descriptive checks with no committed plan |
| Analytic | Follows from the construction or an argument, not an experiment |
| Literature | Rests on cited work |
| Process record | A statement about how the research was done, sourced to commits |
| Open | Not yet supported; must be checked or dropped |
