  PARAGRAPH (DESCRIPTIVE, 2026-10-25). Off-pattern versus general distance. Across the 7 LOCO folds with >= 20 test rows, the fold's
  median off-pattern distance tracks its LOCO RMSE more closely than general distance does (Spearman: co-solvent off-pattern +0.50,
  p 0.25; mixture-level off-pattern +0.68, p 0.094; Mahalanobis +0.07, nearest-neighbour +0.29, fraction outside the hull +0.49),
  but with n = 7 none of these is distinguishable from zero. Within fold A, where polishing hurt (RMSE 0.142 -> 1.026 at mu 1e-6,
  0.335 at 1e-4), the degradation is LARGEST on the rows NEAREST the training data (Spearman of the |error| change with Mahalanobis
  distance -0.70 / -0.57, mixture off-pattern -0.66 / -0.52 at mu 1e-6 / 1e-4). This is not an artefact of the difference measure:
  the polished ensembles' own |error| falls with distance in the same way (-0.72 / -0.60), while the early ensemble's barely does
  (-0.29). Inside fold A every distance is almost collinear with the EA fraction x_co (Spearman -0.97 for Mahalanobis, -1.00 for the
  mixture off-pattern), and KAN-delta predicts x_co * h, so a polish-induced shift in h is magnified where x_co is large; per row,
  distance cannot be separated from that multiplier, and the co-solvent off-pattern distance is constant over fold A (G1) and cannot
  be tested per row at all. Where the held-out sets sit: fold A lies outside the training hull in space B but ON the training
  correlation pattern (co-solvent off-pattern 0.047, among the smallest of all folds); the DEC test rows lie outside the hull and
  about 7 x further off the pattern (0.334; mixture-level 0.254 vs 0.112) and farther in Mahalanobis distance (11.1 vs 6.6; DEC is
  measured against the full anchored set, fold A against its fold's training rows). So these descriptive results do NOT show off-
  pattern distance bearing on the fold A degradation: fold A is an on-pattern fold, and within it the per-row pattern is carried by
  x_co. The parked predictive study's stated precondition is therefore not met by this report; the decision is the author's.
