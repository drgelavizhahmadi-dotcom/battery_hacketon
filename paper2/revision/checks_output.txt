==================== 2. ADAMW WEIGHT DECAY ====================
  kan.py: train(): torch.optim.AdamW(net.parameters(), lr=LR, weight_decay=WD)  (final.py calls kan.train)
  paper2/synth.py: train_early(): torch.optim.AdamW(net.parameters(), lr=LR, weight_decay=WD)
  arch/sc_model.py: train_sc(): torch.optim.AdamW(net.parameters(), lr=LR, weight_decay=WD)
  arch/ex_model.py: train_ex(): torch.optim.AdamW(net.parameters(), lr=LR, weight_decay=WD)
  weight_decay = WD = 0.0001 (kan.py), lr = 0.003; one parameter group = ALL parameters: RBF coefficients, SiLU base weights,
  biases, salt embedding (KAN-delta, SC, EX) and, for SC, A, W, w, b. Also used unchanged by control2/3, rank, compare.
  AdamW's decay is DECOUPLED: each step multiplies every parameter by (1 - lr*wd) outside the Adam update. wd*||theta||^2/2
  below is therefore an EQUIVALENT-LOSS comparison (what an L2 term of that weight would contribute), not the training objective.

==================== 3. PENALTY NORMALISATION ====================

  fiber, fiber2, control2/3, close, certify). Every penalty is a SUM (over all edges, and over coefficients within an edge):
    D2 = sum over edges and second differences of c^2 (kan.KANLayer.smoothness, synth.penalty_terms, close.FlatQ, sc_model.penalty)
    Q  = sum over edges ||P_null c||^2 (+ sum of squared SiLU weights in close/certify), SiLU term = sum alpha^2, beta-term = sum of squares
